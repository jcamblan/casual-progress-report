The fine print

Our ranks: guilds are ranked by Mythic bosses killed, among the guilds currently shown. For equal boss counts, the week in which the guild reached its current progression breaks ties: earlier regional reset weeks rank higher. Guilds reaching the same boss count in the same week share a rank (1, 2, 2, 4). If any visible guild in a progression group has no confirmed week, the whole group has no assigned rank until its weeks are known. Dates are first kills recorded by Raider.io; missing or late updates can make them incomplete or approximate. Casual cred and external ranks do not break ties.

Source ranks: Raider.io regional and world ranks are for this raid on Mythic, as of the progression fetch. WP ranks are copied from the saved WowProgress page, with its original world or EU scope; they are not ranks within this report. Source rankings can use different rules and update at different times. The guild details show their snapshot dates. A missing rank stays unavailable.

A raid night uses the local realm date with a 06:00 cutoff: pulls after midnight but before 06:00 belong to the previous night. Only Mythic boss pulls from this tier count, including wipes and Mythic alt raids. Week boundaries use each region’s reset schedule; EU uses Wednesday at 04:00 UTC and US uses Tuesday at 07:00 Pacific time.

Casual cred % = 100 × completed active weeks with exactly one Mythic night / completed active weeks observed. Weeks with no observed pulls, partial periods and the current week are excluded. More cred means fewer evenings sacrificed to the raid calendar.

Guilds awaiting a complete, recent WCL check remain listed when their Raider.io progression is valid and recent. Raider.io tracked Mythic pulls can provide a provisional score while WCL processes, once the configured minimum number of active weeks is met. Missing tracked pulls cannot prove no raid happened. Use “Verified guilds only” to hide all rows still awaiting WCL.

This report measures accessible public logs attributed to each guild. It cannot rule out private or unlogged raids. Imported WowProgress pages may cover only part of the candidate pool.
